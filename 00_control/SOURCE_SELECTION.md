# Source selection

Decision: admit Wen-Wei Li, Methods in Algebra, Volume 1, as the coherent,
complete organizing core of the selected O013 composite. Retain Milne only as
an English comparator/reference; it is not a translated companion.

Li supplies one integrated progression through categorical foundations,
groups, rings, modules, tensor and algebra constructions, fields and Galois
theory, valuations, completions, local/global fields, and Witt vectors. The
official editable XeLaTeX closure and translation permission are available.

The strongest lawful editable Milne bundle was Group Theory v4.00 plus Fields
and Galois Theory v5.00: 279 pages under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It lacks a coherent
general module/tensor/commutative-algebra spine and substantially duplicates
Judson. Adding Milne's 113-page commutative-algebra primer would make a
392-page bundle, but that component had no public editable source or derivative
permission. It is therefore inadmissible for this translation lane.

Li overlaps undergraduate Judson in parts of Chapters 4-9, but adds the
integrated categorical architecture, modules/tensors, infinite Galois theory,
valuations/completions, local/global fields, and Witt vectors required by D70.
The absence of full solutions is recorded as a support-layer gap, not a reason
to replace the coherent core.

The complete course architecture selected on 2026-08-21 adds the complete MIT
OCW 18.712 Etingof notes plus their separate assessment and only six exact
repaired CRing spans. This supersedes the provisional Milne/Altman–Kleiman
companion recommendation without changing the decision to finish all of Li
Volume 1. Exact authority, scope, rights, repair gates, and production order
are frozen in `O013_COMPOSITE_SELECTION_20260821.md`.
